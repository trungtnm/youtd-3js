How to play
Elements and research

Towers belong to seven elements. Spend knowledge tomes to research an element: higher levels unlock stronger tiers and rarer towers.

Armor matters

Each attack type is strong against one armor and weak against others. Divine armor shrugs off everything except Essence. Check the incoming wave and adapt.

Waves

Mass, normal, air and boss waves arrive with random races and special traits. Every 8th wave is a Challenge: it cannot hurt the portal but pays out for every hit.

Grow your towers

Towers level up from kills, carry items dropped by creeps, and drink oils for permanent power. Combine spare items with Transmute.

Controls

WASD / arrows or screen edges to pan, wheel to zoom, Q/E or middle-drag to rotate, right-drag to pan. Space sends the next wave. U upgrades, X sells, F/G cast the selected tower's abilities (right-click an ability to toggle autocast). Shift keeps placing.

Few towers, deep builds

Untrained towers are weak: level 0 deals 35% damage, level 5 100%, level 25 about 360%. Levels go up to 60, and items multiply on top. Every tower starts with 1 item slot and gains one every 10 waves (6 at wave 50), and picks a perk every 5 levels. Each tower may carry one unique item. Spend spare gold on Train to level your carries.
